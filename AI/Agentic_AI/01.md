# Agentic AI?

* **Simple Lafzon Mein:** Agentic AI ek aisi advanced AI hai jo sirf aapke sawalon ke jawab nahi deti, balkay khud se faislay kar ke poora kaam **complete (execute)** karti hai.

* **Misaal:** Normal AI (jaise ChatGPT) ek **Consultant** ki tarah hai—aap sawal poochte hain, woh mashwara ya text deti hai. Jabke Agentic AI ek **Autonomous Employee** ki tarah hai—aap sirf maqsad (goal) batate hain, aur woh baqi poora kaam khud plan aur execute karti hai.

---

# Normal AI (Generative AI) vs Agentic AI

| Feature | Normal AI (Generative AI) | Agentic AI |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Kaam Ka Tarika** | Text ya answers generate karti hai. | Actions leti hai aur tasks poore karti hai. |
| **Insaan Ka Role** | Har step par insaan ko khud instruction deni parti hai. | Insaan sirf ek baar **Goal** deta hai, baqi AI khud karti hai. |
| **Flight Booking Example** | Aapko Lahore ki flights ki list bana kar de gi, ticket aapko khud book karni pare gi. | Internet par search kare gi, prices compare kare gi, payment kare gi, aur ticket email par bhej de gi. |
| **Output Type** | Answers (Jawab) | Outcomes (Nateeje) |

# Agentic AI Kaise Kaam Karti Hai?

Agentic AI kisi bhi kaam ko poora karne ka liya 4 steps follow karta hai:

* **Step 1: Goal Understanding (Maqsad Samajhna)**
* AI aapke diye gaye task ka maqsad aur uski limits (rules) samajhti hai.
* *Misaal:* Agar aap kahein "Laptop dhoond kar do", toh woh pehle samajhti hai ke budget kya hai aur kaun se features zaroori hain.


* **Step 2: Planning & Task Breakdown (Kaam Ko Chote Steps Mein Torhna)**
* Bade kaam ko chote, sequential steps mein taqseem karti hai.
* *Misaal:* Step 1: Specs comparison -> Step 2: Reviews read karna -> Step 3: Best price dhoondna -> Step 4: Purchase link generate karna.


* **Step 3: Execution & Tool Use (Tools Ka Istemal)**
* Zaroorat ke mutabiq alag-alag digital tools (Web Search, APIs, Python Code, Databases, Excel) ko khud chalati hai.
* *Misaal:* Python code chala kar internet se real-time prices scrape karna ya database mein entry karna.


* **Step 4: Self-Reflection & Re-try (Khud Ko Check Karna)**
* Agar koi step fail ho jaye (e.g., koi website na khule ya code mein error aaye), toh AI rukti nahi hai. Woh error ko samajh kar apna rasta badalti hai aur dobara koshish karti hai.

---

**4. Real-Life Examples (Har Field Mein Istemal)**

* **Software Engineering & Coding:**
* *Kaam:* Developer sirf kehta hai "Login page par Google Sign-In button lagao."
* *Agentic Role:* AI khud code likhti hai, files update karti hai, bugs test karti hai, aur code server par **deploy** kar deti hai.

---

**5. Agentic AI Ka Asli Purpose (Maqsad)**

Agentic AI ka sab se bara maqsad insaanon ko **repetitive, multi-step, aur time-consuming digital tasks** se azaad karna hai. Normal AI aapko *rasta batati hai*, jabke Agentic AI us *raste par khud chal kar manzil tak puhanchati hai*.
