# What is Order Block:
1. OB aik Candle ho sekhti hai yeh aik Zone ho sekhta hai jahan se institutions , Banks , Big Players ya smart money ne apne large orders place kiye hote hain.
2. Jiski waja se Price aik Impulsive move dyta hai yeah tu Upside yeah Downside. 
3. Jab aik bara Amount Market mein Enter hota hai tu waha per Impulsive move ata hai jiski waja se aik Imbalance Create hojata hai jisa hum fvg bolte hai.
4. Ager Chart mein Impulsive move or proper Imbalance create hota hai tu waha ka OB valid mana jata hai.
5. Aik OB ko find karne se phela Liquidity Sweep hona zarori hai jab tak market Liquidity Sweep nahe karti hai hume Trade mein Enter nahe hona chaiya or na he Sweep hone se phela OB ko find karna chaiya.

# Mitigation in Order Block:
1. Mitigation Block ka matlab hota hai ek aisi jagah (ya area) chart par, jahan pe pehle market ne koi strong move kiya ho – jaise upar ya neeche – aur us jagah pe ek Order Block bana ho.

2. Lekin jab market dobara usi jagah pe aata hai (us block ko retest karta hai), aur wahan se fir se move karta hai – to hum kehte hain ke wo mitigate ho gaya. Iska matlab ye hota hai ke market ne us jagah se jo imbalance tha, ya jo orders bache hue the, unko pura kar diya hai.


Bullish Order Block: Yeh tab banta hai jab market pehle neeche ki taraf jata hai aur logon ke stop losses ko hit karta hai (yaani neeche liquidity leta hai). Uske baad market wapas upar ki taraf strong move karta hai aur jis red candle (ya candles) ke through neeche gaya tha, uski opening price se upar close deta hai. Iska matlab yeh hota hai ke market ne neeche stop loss hunt kiya aur ab buyers control mein aa gaye hain.

Bearish Order Block: Yeh tab banta hai jab market pehle upar ki taraf jata hai aur wahan par stop losses hit karta hai (yaani upar liquidity leta hai). Uske baad market neeche ki taraf strong move karta hai aur jis green candle (ya candles) ke through upar gaya tha, uski opening price se neeche close deta hai. Iska matlab hota hai ke market ne upar stop loss hunt kiya aur ab sellers control mein aa gaye hain.
