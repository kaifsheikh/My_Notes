# What is Support and Resistance:

| Feature        | Support                | Resistance                 |
| -------------- | ---------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Location       | Neeche                 | Upar                       |
| Role           | Price ko girne se roke | Price ko upar jane se roke |
| Power          | Buyers (Demand)        | Sellers (Supply)           |
| Market Feeling | Cheap                  | Expensive                  |
| Action         | Bounce up              | Rejection down             |

# Strong Support Resistance vs Weak Support Resistance:
1. Strong or Weak find karne se phela sirf itna dekhna hota hai ka jab price kisi level per ay chahay wo Support ho yeah Resistance ois Level per ane ka bad Price kis terha se `React` kar reha hai oisko samajne ka bad he hum decide karta hai ka wo Level Strong hai yeah Weak.
3. aysa Level jis Level ka neecha koi dosra Support na ho yeah kaafi bara Gap ka bad dosra Support ho yeah koi aysa Level jis level ka Uper koi dosra Rsistance na ho yeah kaafi bara Gap ka bad dosre Resistance ho tu wo Strong Support or Resistance hote hai

| Feature           | Strong S/R      | Weak S/R      |
| ----------------- | --------------- | ------------- |
| Touches           | Multiple (2–4+) | Single / Few  |
| Timeframe         | Highest TF      | Lower TF      |
| Rejection         | Strong candles  | Weak Candles  |
| Volume            | High Vol        | Low Vol       |
| Break Probability | Low             | High          |

# Trading Types:

| Trading Type | Definition (Short & Strong)         | Timeframe Used | Holding Time      |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------- | -------------- | ----------------- |
| **Scalping** | Very fast trading for small profits | 1M – 5M        | Seconds – Minutes |
| **Intraday** | Buy & sell within the same day      | 5M – 30M       | Same Day          |
| **Swing**    | Capture short-term market swings    | 1H – 1D        | Days – Weeks      |
| **Position** | Trade long-term major trends        | 1D – 1W        | Weeks – Months    |
