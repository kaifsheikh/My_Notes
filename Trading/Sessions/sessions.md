# 🔹 Forex Market 24 hours kyun chalta hai?

Forex kisi ek exchange par depend nahi karta — yeh duniya bhar ke financial centers par chalta hai:

- Tokyo
- London
- New York

👉 Jab ek market band hota hai, dusra open ho jata hai
👉 Isliye 24 hours trading hoti hai

---

# 🔥 4 Main Forex Sessions (Pakistani Time ke mutabiq)

## 1️⃣ Sydney Session (Sabse pehla)

- 🕒 **PKT: 5:00 AM – 2:00 PM**
- 🔹 Slow market
- 🔹 Low volatility
- 🔹 Beginners ke liye acha nahi

---

## 2️⃣ Tokyo Session (Asian Session)

- 🕒 **PKT: 6:00 AM – 3:00 PM**
- 🔹 Moderate movement
- 🔹 Yen pairs active (JPY)

👉 Best pairs:

- USDJPY
- EURJPY

💡 Market stable hota hai, zyada sharp moves nahi hoti

---

## 3️⃣ London Session (Sabse powerful 🔥)

- 🕒 **PKT: 1:00 PM – 10:00 PM**
- 🔹 High volatility
- 🔹 Big movements
- 🔹 Smart money entry

👉 Best pairs:

- EURUSD
- GBPUSD

💡 **Yahan se real trading start hoti hai**

---

## 4️⃣ New York Session

- 🕒 **PKT: 6:00 PM – 3:00 AM**
- 🔹 Strong moves
- 🔹 News impact zyada

👉 USD related pairs sabse zyada move karte hain

---

# 🔥 Sabse Important (Overlap Time)

## London + New York overlap

- 🕒 **PKT: 6:00 PM – 10:00 PM**

👉 **Yeh GOLDEN TIME hai** 💰
👉 Sabse zyada volatility
👉 Sabse zyada profit opportunities

---

# 🔴 Har session mein kya hota hai (real trader insight)

## 🟢 Asian (Tokyo)

- Market range mein hota hai
- Breakouts fake hote hain
- Scalping possible

---

## 🔵 London

- Strong trend start hota hai
- Previous range break hoti hai
- Big institutions enter karte hain

---

## 🔴 New York

- Trend continue ya reverse hota hai
- News impact (USD news)
- High volatility spikes

---

# 🔹 Best Trading Time (Pakistan)

👉 Agar tum serious ho trading mein:

🔥 **Best time:**

- 1:00 PM – 4:00 PM (London start)
- 6:00 PM – 10:00 PM (London + NY overlap) ⭐

# 🔹 Strategy level understanding

👉 Smart traders kya karte hain:

- Asian session → range mark karte hain
- London open → breakout trade
- NY → confirmation ya reversal
