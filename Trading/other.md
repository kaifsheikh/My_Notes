## What is `High Liquidity` and `Low Liquidity`?
1. jitne zeyada market mein Buyers or Sellers honge oisa hum `High Liquidity` bolte hai Hight Liquidity mein Order jaldi fill hote hai koi issue nahe hota hai or Spread bhe kam hota hai.

2. jitne kam Buyers or Sellers Market mein honge oisa hum `Low Liquidity` bolte hai Low Liquidity mein Order jaldi fill nahe hote hai isme issue hota hai or Spread bhe zeyada hota hai.
