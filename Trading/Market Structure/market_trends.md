# Type of Trend

# BOS
1. `Break of Structure (BOS)` ka matlab hai jab market apne current trend ko continou rakhay chahe wo `Uptrend` ho yeah `Downtrend`
2. lakin asal sawal tu yeah hai ka `Real BOS` kab hota hai jisa hume yeah idea ho ka kia market ka trend abhe bhe `Continue` hai yeah phir `Change` hogaya hai oiska liya hume real BOS ko find karna hota hai.
3. Uptrend mein BOS `High Break` hone se hota hai.
4. or Downtrend mein BOS `Low Break` hone se hota hai.
5. yeah yaad rekho ka BOS humesha `Body Close` hone per hota hai chahe Makret Uptend mein ho yeah Downtrend mein.
6. ager market Uptrend mein hai or wo apne `Previous High` ko Break karte hai or ois High ka uper `Body Close` karti hai tu oisko hum Real `BOS` bolte hai.
7. isi terha se ager market Downtrend mein hai or market apne `Previous Low` ko Break karke `Body` neecha Close karti hai tu wo Real `BOS` hoga.

4. ab hum aik point ko or ache se samajte hai jisa retracement bolte hai jisko Pullback bhe bola jata hai iska relation humera pure Market Struture , BOS etc sub per depend karta hai.

# Retracement
1. `Uptrend mein:` Jab market oopar ja rahi ho aur jab koi Candle apne bilkul recent wali Candle ka Low ko Break kare chahe wo ois Low ka neecha Candle CLose kare yeah sirf Wick bana kar Sweep kare or uper close karde dono sourto mein yeah Pullback kaylayga lakin yeah Confirm Pullback nhe kaylayga jab yeah Market ka Recent High Break nahe hota hai Body ois recent ka uper close nahe hoti hai jab tak Pullback Confirm nahe hota hai jaise he price apne recent High ko Break karke Body uper close karti hai tu ois Swing ka subse Lowest Point he Humera Real Retracement khalayga or oiske Lowest Point ko hum `IDM` bhe bolte hai.

2. `Downtrend mein:` Jab market neeche ja rahi ho aur koi candle apne se pichli candle ka High tod de, to woh "Valid Pullback" hai.

# CHoCH:
1. `Change of Character (CHoCH)` ka matlab hai jab market apne current trend ko continou na rakhay chahe wo `Uptrend` ho yeah `Downtrend` tu oisa hum Choch bolte hai.
2. lakin asal sawal tu yeah hai ka `Real CHoCH` kab hota hai jisa hume yeah idea ho ka kia market ka trend abhe bhe `Continue` hai yeah phir `Change` hogaya hai oiska liya hume real CHoCH ko find karna hota hai.
3. Uptrend mein CHoCH `Low Break` hone se hota hai.
4. or Downtrend mein CHoCH `High Break` hone se hota hai.
5. yeah yaad rekho ka CHoCH humesha `Body Close` hone per hota hai chahe Makret Uptend mein ho yeah Downtrend mein.
6. ager market Uptrend mein hai or wo apne `Previous Low` ko Break karte hai or ois Low ka neecha `Body Close` karti hai tu oisko hum Real `CHoCH` bolte hai.
7. isi terha se ager market Downtrend mein hai or market apne `Previous High` ko Break karke `Body` uper Close karti hai tu wo Real `CHoCH` hoga.
