# What is SDLC:

1. **SDLC** means **Software Development Life Cycle**.

2. yeh woh complete way hai jo aik software ko start se lekar end stage market mein launch honay tak tay karna parta hai.

3. jaise ghar bananay se phela oiska pura blueprint ready hota hai bilkul waise hi aik software bananay ka bhi poora process hota hai jise SDLC kehte hain.

# Purpose:

* **Step by step process:** Team ko pata hota hai ke ab agla kaam kya karna hai.
* **Mistakes ko identify karna:** Kaam ko steps mein baantne se ghaltiyan shuru mein hi pakar li jati hain.
* **Waqt aur Budget bachana:** Sab kuch plan ke mutabiq chalta hai toh project waqt par aur kam kharch mein mukammal ho jata hai.

---

## Step 1: Requirement Gathering & Analysis:

* **means:** Sab se pehle socha jata hai ke software mein kya kya hona chahiye. Client batata hai ke usko kya chahiye.
* **example:** Restaurant wala kahega ke mujhe aik aisi app chahiye jahan log khana order kar sakein aur online payment kar sakein.

## Step 2: Design:

* **means:** Software kaisa dikhega aur iska andar ka system kaise kaam karega, iska blueprint banaya jata hai.
* **example:** Designer aik screen banata hai ke button kahan hoga, menu kahan nazar aayega, aur color konsa hoga (UI/UX design).

## Step 3: Implementation / Coding:

* **means:** Developers apna dimag lagate hain aur code likhna shuru karte hain taake app ban jaye.
* **example:** Programmers app ke buttons aur database ko coding ke zariye zinda karte hain.

## Step 4: Testing - **Yahan aapka SQA ka kaam shuru hota hai!**

* **means:** Jab app ban jati hai, toh QA Testers usko chala kar dekhte hain ke kahin koi bug ya kharabi toh nahi hai.
* **example:** Tester check karta hai ke order button dabane par order restaurant tak ja raha hai ya nahi, ya payment cut ho rahi hai ya nahi. Agar koi ghalti ho toh developers ko wapas bhej di jati hai.

## Step 5: Deployment:

* **means:** Jab app bilkul theek ho jati hai, toh isko aam logon ke liye Play Store ya App Store par dal diya jata hai.
* **example:** Ab aam log Google Play Store se is app ko download kar sakte hain.

## Step 6: Maintenance (Sambhalna aur Update Rakhna)

* **means:** App launch honay ke baad bhi agar koi masla aaye ya nayi cheezein add karni hon, toh team usko update karti rehti hai.
* **example:** App mein naye features dalna ya koi chota bug fix karna.
